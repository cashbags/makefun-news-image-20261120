India — Lead story

Cyclone Akvan strikes India’s east coast

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan comes ashore around noon near Gopalpur, on the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha border, with sustained winds of 90–100 km/h gusting to 110. Odisha has 1,012 emergency teams in the field and shut schools in six districts as heavy rain spreads across both states.

LANDFALL between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur, about noon.  WINDS 90–100 km/h, gusts to 110 km/h.  RAIN heavy-rain alerts in 19 districts of south-east Odisha and northern Andhra Pradesh.

The Hague — Justice

World court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide

The International Court of Justice rules, 9–6, that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 — the first time in the court’s 80-year history that a state has been held responsible for genocide. It orders reparations and the safe return of survivors; the world reacts today.

Ukraine

Russian strikes hit Austrian and Dutch embassies in Kyiv

Russian strikes on the Ukrainian capital damage the Austrian and Dutch embassies, the day’s top story from the war. Moscow says it will not negotiate with Kyiv after the Crimean Bridge strikes; Trump tells European leaders Putin wants the war to end.

Shenzhen — Fallout

The “peace declaration” names no war at all

A day after Trump, Xi and Putin sign their four-point declaration in Shenzhen, the world reads the small print: it never mentions Ukraine, Iran or North Korea. Macron says it will not “reorganise the world”; Trump holds a White House press conference today.

South Korea

Court clears ex-PM Han over martial law case

South Korea’s Supreme Court overturns the conviction of former prime minister Han Duck-soo in the case linked to the 2024 martial law declaration — closing one chapter of the crisis that shook the country two years ago.

Fiji

Fiji declares a national disaster as drought bites

Fiji declares a national state of disaster as severe drought and widespread water shortages, driven by El Niño, grip the island nation — the Pacific’s latest climate emergency.
